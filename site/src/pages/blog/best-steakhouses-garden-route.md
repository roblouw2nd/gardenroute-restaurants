South Africans take their grills seriously, and the Garden Route delivers everything from precision-cooked town-centre steaks to bushveld lodge dining. Here's where to find a great one, with the [full steakhouse list](/eat/steakhouse).

## The standout

[Steak by Reel and Rustic](/steak-by-reel-and-rustic-george) in [George](/town/george) is the route's steak benchmark — quality cuts cooked properly in the historic town centre. If you care about your steak, start here.

## Lodge and reserve dining

For a steak with an experience attached, [Botlierskop](/botlierskop-tented-lodge-glentana) near [Klein Brak River](/town/klein-brak-river) serves African-inspired cuisine amid a private game reserve, and [Botlierskop's Spekbos Restaurant](/botlierskop-day-visitors-spekbos-restaurant-mossel-bay) welcomes day visitors. In [Great Brak River](/town/great-brak-river), [Transkaroo](/transkaroo-restaurant-mossel-bay) does classic Karoo-style cooking with hearty portions.

## Dependable family grills

When you want a reliable, family-friendly grill, the Spur chain is everywhere along the route — [Geronimo Spur](/geronimo-spur-george) in George and [Lynx Creek Spur](/lynx-creek-spur-mossel-bay) in [Hartenbos](/town/hartenbos) are solid, kid-friendly options.

## Heritage settings

For atmosphere, [Die Stasie](/die-stasie-hartenbos-mossel-bay) — a converted railway station in Hartenbos — and the country-style [Die Stal](/die-stal-restaurant-george) between George and Great Brak both pair hearty South African plates with character.
