The Garden Route is one of South Africa's great road trips, and the eating is every bit as good as the scenery. Stretching roughly 300km from Mossel Bay in the west to Storms River in the east, the route packs in lagoon-side oyster bars, forest farm stalls, beachfront seafood shacks and genuinely excellent small-town cafés. This guide pulls together the best places to eat in each town, so you can plan your meals as carefully as your stops.

We list [over 400 restaurants across 13 towns](/browse), each with current ratings, hours and directions. Below is the short version — the standouts worth building a day around.

## Where to eat, town by town

**[Knysna](/town/knysna)** is the heart of the route. It's famous for oysters, but the food scene runs much deeper — [East Head Café](/east-head-cafe-knysna-knysna) for breakfast over the lagoon mouth, [Île de Pain](/browse?q=ile%20de%20pain) for legendary bakery fare on Thesen Islands, and [Knysna Gin Distillery & Cocktailbar](/knysna-gin-distillery-cocktailbar-knysna) for something livelier.

**[Plettenberg Bay](/town/plettenberg-bay)** is the upmarket beach town. [Emily Moon](/emily-moon-plettenberg-bay) is the special-occasion favourite, with views over the Bitou River, while [Nice Neighbour Cafe](/nice-neighbour-cafe-plettenberg-bay) and [Café Civara](/cafe-civara-plettenberg-bay) handle the everyday brilliantly.

**[Mossel Bay](/town/mossel-bay)** mixes heritage charm with harbour-fresh seafood and a surprisingly strong coffee culture — start with [Bean Blessed Coffee](/bean-blessed-coffee-mossel-bay) or clifftop views at [Pinnacle Point](/pinnacle-point-restaurant-mossel-bay).

**[George](/town/george)**, the route's biggest town, is where you'll find the widest variety: serious steaks at [Steak by Reel and Rustic](/steak-by-reel-and-rustic-george), wood-fired pizza at [K-risp](/k-risp-pizza-george), and fine dining at [The Deacon House](/the-deacon-house-george).

**[Wilderness](/town/wilderness)** is all about relaxed, nature-immersed eating — [The Commonage](/the-commonage-wilderness), [Mozambik](/mozambik-wilderness-wilderness) for peri-peri, and roadside oysters at [Oysters R Us](/oysters-r-us-wilderness-garden-route-wilderness).

**[Sedgefield](/town/sedgefield)**, South Africa's first "Slow Town", punches above its weight — try plant-based [Liola Vegan Kitchen](/liola-vegan-kitchen-sedgefield) or the Saturday Scarab Village vibe at [Mojo Café](/mojo-cafe-sedgefield-sedgefield).

**[Storms River](/town/storms-river)** and the Tsitsikamma are your last stop east — hearty forest meals and [Fynboshoek Cheese](/fynboshoek-cheese-storms-river), a farm experience worth the detour.

## When to visit

The Garden Route eats well year-round, but summer (December–February) is peak — book ahead for dinner in Plett and Knysna. Winter is quieter, cheaper and arguably lovelier, with misty forests and empty beaches. The [Knysna Oyster Festival](/blog/knysna-oysters-where-to-eat) each July is the single busiest food week of the year.
