The Garden Route is made for driving, and half the fun is knowing where to pull over. This west-to-east itinerary maps the best places to eat along the N2, so you never settle for a forgettable petrol-station pie. Adjust the pace to suit — it's a brilliant three days, or a packed long weekend.

## Stop 1 — Mossel Bay (the gateway)

Start with coffee at [Bean Blessed](/bean-blessed-coffee-mossel-bay) in [Mossel Bay](/town/mossel-bay), then a clifftop lunch at [Pinnacle Point](/pinnacle-point-restaurant-mossel-bay). Just east, detour to [De Vette Mossel](/de-vette-mossel-grootbrak-beach-seafood-restaurant-experience-mossel-bay) at [Great Brak River](/town/great-brak-river) for a beach seafood feast.

## Stop 2 — George & the bays

[George](/town/george) is the place for a proper dinner — steak at [Steak by Reel and Rustic](/steak-by-reel-and-rustic-george) or pizza at [K-risp](/k-risp-pizza-george). Detour to [Victoria Bay](/town/victoria-bay) for ocean views at [Hilltop](/hilltop-boutique-hotel-victoria-bay).

## Stop 3 — Wilderness & Sedgefield

Slow down. Coffee at [The Commonage](/the-commonage-wilderness) in [Wilderness](/town/wilderness), oysters at roadside [Oysters R Us](/oysters-r-us-wilderness-garden-route-wilderness), then a market morning in [Sedgefield](/town/sedgefield) — grab a bite at [Mojo Café](/mojo-cafe-sedgefield-sedgefield).

## Stop 4 — Knysna (the midpoint)

Base yourself in [Knysna](/town/knysna). Breakfast at [East Head Café](/east-head-cafe-knysna-knysna), oysters by the lagoon (see our [oyster guide](/blog/knysna-oysters-where-to-eat)), and dinner with cocktails at [Knysna Gin Distillery](/knysna-gin-distillery-cocktailbar-knysna).

## Stop 5 — Plettenberg Bay

In [Plett](/town/plettenberg-bay), do a long lunch at [Bramon Wine Estate](/bramon-wine-estate-plettenberg-bay) on The Crags and a sunset dinner at [Emily Moon](/emily-moon-plettenberg-bay).

## Stop 6 — Storms River (the finale)

End in the forest. Visit [Fynboshoek Cheese](/fynboshoek-cheese-storms-river) in the Tsitsikamma and hearty meals at [Tilly's](/tsitsikamma-gardens-self-catering-cottages-tilly-s-restaurant-storms-river). See the [full Storms River guide](/blog/best-restaurants-storms-river-tsitsikamma).
