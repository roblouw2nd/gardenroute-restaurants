The Garden Route has quietly become one of South Africa's best coffee regions, with small-batch roasters and serious baristas hiding in unexpected villages. Whether you're fuelling a road trip or settling in for a slow morning, here's where to find a proper cup — and the [full coffee-shop list](/eat/coffee-shop).

## The roasters

[Bean Blessed Coffee](/bean-blessed-coffee-mossel-bay) in [Mossel Bay](/town/mossel-bay) is an artisan roastery worth a detour. In [Great Brak River](/town/great-brak-river), [Brothers Coffee](/brothers-coffee-hole-in-the-wall-outlet-groot-brak-river) roasts its own, and [Pause Coffee @ The Barn on Montagu](/pause-coffee-the-barn-on-montagu-george) in [George](/town/george) does specialty coffee in a charming barn.

## Village favourites

[Wilderness](/town/wilderness) is spoilt: [Peron CoffeeBar](/peron-coffeebar-wilderness) is a cosy station stop, and [The Commonage](/the-commonage-wilderness) anchors the community. In [Sedgefield](/town/sedgefield), [Flat Lizard & Co](/flat-lizard-co-sedgefield) pairs quality roasts with a deli counter. Over in [Plett](/town/plettenberg-bay), [Eleven Coffee Shop](/eleven-coffee-shop-plett-plettenberg-bay) and [Café Civara](/cafe-civara-plettenberg-bay) cover the Main Street crowd.

## Central, in town

In [Knysna](/town/knysna), [The Daily Coffee Café](/the-daily-coffee-cafe-knysna-knysna) and the art-filled [Art Café on Queen](/art-cafe-on-queen-knysna) are reliable central picks. In Mossel Bay, [Market Cafe](/market-cafe-mossel-bay) and [Coffee@Work](/coffee-work-oppipunt-mossel-bay) keep the harbour town caffeinated.
