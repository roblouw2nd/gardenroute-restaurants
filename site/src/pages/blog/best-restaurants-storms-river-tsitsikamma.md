Storms River marks the eastern end of the Garden Route, where the forest gets denser, the air cools and the Tsitsikamma's famous suspension bridge and canopy tours draw adventurers. It's a small village, but it eats surprisingly well. Here's where to go, with the [full Storms River list](/town/storms-river) for everything.

## Don't-miss experiences

[Fynboshoek Cheese](/fynboshoek-cheese-storms-river) is the area's standout — a family-run artisan cheese farm deep in the Tsitsikamma offering tastings and farm-fresh platters. It's a genuine destination, not just a meal. For something completely different, [Kasi Corner](/kasi-corner-storms-river) serves authentic township cuisine and traditional South African dishes with real warmth.

## Hearty forest meals

After a day on the trails you'll want comfort food. [Tsitsikamma Gardens' Tilly's Restaurant](/tsitsikamma-gardens-self-catering-cottages-tilly-s-restaurant-storms-river) does hearty home-cooked meals amid the forest, while [Misty Mountain Reserve](/misty-mountain-reserve-lodge-and-self-catering-chalets-storms-river) feeds hikers with generous mountain-lodge fare. For pizza and craft beer, [Tsitsikamma Micro Brewery](/tsitsikamma-micro-brewery-storms-river) hits the spot.

## On the coast

If you make it down to the rugged shoreline, [Storms River Mouth Restcamp](/storms-river-mouth-restcamp-storms-river-mouth-rest-camp-storms-river) inside the Garden Route National Park has ocean-view dining beside the dramatic river mouth — one of the most spectacular settings on the route.

## Nearby

Storms River pairs naturally with a day trip from [Plettenberg Bay](/town/plettenberg-bay) (about 45 minutes west) and the quiet beauty of [Nature's Valley](/town/natures-valley). It's the perfect grand finale — or starting point — for a Garden Route trip.
