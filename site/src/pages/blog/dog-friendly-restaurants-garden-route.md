The Garden Route is one of the most dog-friendly corners of South Africa — endless beaches, forest walks and, happily, plenty of restaurants where your dog is welcome at the table (or under it). Here's where to bring them, with the [full dog-friendly list](/eat/dog-friendly).

## Breweries and beer gardens

Beer gardens are a dog's best friend. [Red Bridge Brewing Co](/red-bridge-brewing-co-knysna) in [Knysna](/town/knysna) has a relaxed, dog-welcoming garden with wood-fired pizza, and [Elvis Brew Co](/elvis-brew-co-george) near [George](/town/george) airport offers craft beer and space to roam.

## Wine estates and farm stalls

[Bramon Wine Estate](/bramon-wine-estate-plettenberg-bay) near [Plett](/town/plettenberg-bay) welcomes dogs among the vines, and working farms like [Misty Valley Farm](/misty-valley-farm-mossel-bay) outside [Mossel Bay](/town/mossel-bay) and [Klipheuwel Farmstall](/klipheuwel-farmstall-glentana) in [Klein Brak River](/town/klein-brak-river) are made for muddy paws.

## Cafés that welcome dogs

In [Sedgefield](/town/sedgefield), [Flat Lizard & Co](/flat-lizard-co-sedgefield) and [Liquid & Leaf](/liquid-leaf-liqid-new-location-sedgefield) are both happy to see dogs, and the surf café [New Wave Village](/new-wave-village-cafe-victoria-bay) in [Victoria Bay](/town/victoria-bay) is as laid-back as it gets.

## A few quick tips

Always keep dogs leashed unless told otherwise, bring water, and check ahead in peak season when patios get busy. Many Garden Route beaches allow dogs off-peak — pair a beach walk with a meal nearby.
