Mossel Bay is the western gateway to the Garden Route — a working harbour town with a sunny microclimate, big ocean views and a coffee culture that quietly rivals anywhere on the route. Here's where to eat, with the [full Mossel Bay list](/town/mossel-bay) a click away.

## Coffee and breakfast

Mossel Bay takes its coffee seriously. [Bean Blessed Coffee](/bean-blessed-coffee-mossel-bay) is an artisan roastery worth a special trip, while [Market Cafe](/market-cafe-mossel-bay) and [Coffee@Work on Point Road](/coffee-work-oppipunt-mossel-bay) cover central breakfasts and sea-facing tables. For hearty, home-style cooking and generous portions, locals point you to [Carola Ann's](/carola-ann-s-mossel-bay) on Marsh Street.

## Ocean views and seafood

The Point is where you want to be at sunset. [Pinnacle Point Restaurant](/pinnacle-point-restaurant-mossel-bay) offers clifftop dining with sweeping views, and [Royal Blue Lounge](/royal-blue-lounge-cafe-mossel-bay) blends coffee, cocktails and a relaxed central vibe. For the full beach-seafood experience, drive a few minutes to [De Vette Mossel](/de-vette-mossel-grootbrak-beach-seafood-restaurant-experience-mossel-bay) in nearby [Great Brak River](/town/great-brak-river) — a feet-in-the-sand feast of fresh linefish and mussels.

## Family and casual

Mossel Bay is great for families. [Lynx Creek Spur](/lynx-creek-spur-mossel-bay) in [Hartenbos](/town/hartenbos) is a dependable flame-grilled favourite, and beachfront [Ilmazzo](/ilmazzo-mossel-bay) does authentic Italian with ocean views. See more [family-friendly restaurants](/eat/family-friendly).

## Worth the short drive

The Mossel Bay area folds in some lovely satellite villages: [Hartenbos](/town/hartenbos), [Great Brak River](/town/great-brak-river) and [Klein Brak River](/town/klein-brak-river) all have hidden gems, from farm stalls to riverside fish and chips.

## Continue east

From Mossel Bay it's a 40-minute hop to [George](/town/george) and the start of the forest belt. Browse by [seafood](/eat/seafood), [sea views](/eat/sea-view) or [coffee shops](/eat/coffee-shop), or see the [full Garden Route guide](/blog/best-restaurants-garden-route).
