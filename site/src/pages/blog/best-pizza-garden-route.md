Pizza is the Garden Route's great equaliser — found everywhere from wine estates to craft breweries to dedicated wood-fired joints. Here are the bases worth seeking out, with the [full pizza list](/eat/pizza) for everything else.

## Wood-fired specialists

[K-risp Pizza](/k-risp-pizza-george) in Denneoord, [George](/town/george) has a cult following for its crispy, properly wood-fired bases. For an Italian sit-down with pizza and handmade pasta, [Polpetta Ristorante](/polpetta-ristorante-george-george) and [Portobello Italian Kitchen](/portobello-italian-kitchen-george) are both George standouts.

## Beachfront and views

In [Hartenbos](/town/hartenbos), beachfront [Ilmazzo](/ilmazzo-mossel-bay) does authentic Italian with ocean views, and the surf-café [New Wave Village](/new-wave-village-cafe-victoria-bay) in [Victoria Bay](/town/victoria-bay) serves pizza with good vibes right by the break.

## Pizza + a pint

The Garden Route's breweries make some of its best pizza. [Red Bridge Brewing Co](/red-bridge-brewing-co-knysna) in [Knysna](/town/knysna) pairs wood-fired pies with house beer in a dog-friendly garden, and [Tsitsikamma Micro Brewery](/tsitsikamma-micro-brewery-storms-river) does the same in [Storms River](/town/storms-river).

## Pizza + wine

For something more scenic, [Bramon Wine Estate](/bramon-wine-estate-plettenberg-bay) near [Plett](/town/plettenberg-bay) serves pizza and tapas under the vines.
