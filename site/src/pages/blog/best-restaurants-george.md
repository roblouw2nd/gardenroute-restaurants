George is the commercial hub of the Garden Route, and with size comes choice. If you want the broadest spread of cuisines on the route — serious steaks, handmade pasta, Greek street food, boutique-hotel fine dining — this is the town. Here are the picks, with the [full George directory](/town/george) for everything else.

## Steak and grills

[Steak by Reel and Rustic](/steak-by-reel-and-rustic-george) sets the standard for steak in George's historic centre — quality cuts, done properly. For a family-friendly American-style option, [Geronimo Spur](/geronimo-spur-george) is a dependable standby. Browse more [steakhouses](/eat/steakhouse).

## Italian and pizza

George does Italian well. [Polpetta Ristorante](/polpetta-ristorante-george-george) is an authentic trattoria with handmade pasta and wood-fired pizza, while [Portobello Italian Kitchen](/portobello-italian-kitchen-george) covers the classics in George Central. For pizza specifically, [K-risp](/k-risp-pizza-george) in Denneoord has a cult following for its crispy bases — see all [pizza spots](/eat/pizza).

## Fine dining and special meals

For a refined evening, [The Hawthorn Boutique Hotel](/the-hawthorn-boutique-hotel-george) in Heatherlands and the historic [Deacon House](/the-deacon-house-george) both deliver elevated South African cooking. They're among the best [date-night](/eat/date-night) options in town.

## Coffee, casual and something different

Start the day at [Pause Coffee @ The Barn on Montagu](/pause-coffee-the-barn-on-montagu-george), grab Greek street food at [Orexi](/orexi-greek-street-food-george-george), or bring the kids to [Clay Café](/clay-cafe-george-family-restaurant-and-ceramic-painting-george) to paint ceramics over lunch. Beer lovers should find [Elvis Brew Co](/elvis-brew-co-george) near the airport for craft beer and hearty pub fare.

## Sea views nearby

George itself is inland, but [Victoria Bay](/town/victoria-bay) and [Herolds Bay](/town/herolds-bay) are 15–20 minutes away — [Hilltop Boutique Hotel](/hilltop-boutique-hotel-victoria-bay) above Vic Bay's famous surf break is a standout for [ocean-view dining](/eat/sea-view).

## Onward

George is the natural base for the central route, close to [Wilderness](/town/wilderness) and [Mossel Bay](/town/mossel-bay). Read the [full Garden Route guide](/blog/best-restaurants-garden-route) to plan the rest.
