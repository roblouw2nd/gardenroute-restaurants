The Garden Route takes its burgers more seriously than you'd expect from a coastline famous for oysters and fish. Over 80 restaurants on the route serve a proper burger — these are the ones worth planning around.

## Sedgefield: the unlikely burger capital

For a town of its size, Sedgefield's burger bench is absurdly deep. [Kahuna's](/kahuna-s-sedgefield) leads with American-style comfort food and serious coffee (4.8★ from nearly 100 reviews), while [Liquid & Leaf](/liquid-leaf-liqid-new-location-sedgefield) pairs its burgers with craft drinks at the same rating. [Harry's Bar & Diner](/harry-s-bar-diner-sedgefield) covers the classic diner angle, and [Legends Sports Pub](/legends-sports-pub-sedgefield) does lakeside pub grub with Groenvlei views. Even the vegans are covered: [Liola Vegan Kitchen](/liola-vegan-kitchen-sedgefield) makes a loaded plant-based burger that carries a 5★ average.

## Knysna: burgers with a gin chaser

The surprise hit in Knysna is the [Knysna Gin Distillery & Cocktailbar](/knysna-gin-distillery-cocktailbar-knysna) — a working craft distillery in the industrial quarter that happens to serve some of the best burgers in town, backed by 4.8★ across more than 300 reviews. Order a gin cocktail, stay for the patty.

## George and the west

George's size means burger options everywhere from family steakhouses to craft kitchens — browse [burgers in George](/town/george/burgers) for the full field. Towards Mossel Bay, [Lynx Creek Spur](/lynx-creek-spur-mossel-bay) in Hartenbos is the dependable family option — flame-grilled, kid-proof, 4.8★.
