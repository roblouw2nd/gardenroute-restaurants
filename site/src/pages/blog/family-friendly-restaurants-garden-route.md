The Garden Route is a brilliant family holiday, and mealtimes don't have to be a battle. From restaurants with play areas to farm settings where kids can roam, here's where families eat happily — and the [full family-friendly list](/eat/family-friendly).

## Something to do while you eat

The genius pick is [Clay Café](/clay-cafe-george-family-restaurant-and-ceramic-painting-george) in [George](/town/george), where kids paint ceramics while you enjoy lunch. Farm settings also keep little ones busy — [Misty Valley Farm](/misty-valley-farm-mossel-bay) near [Mossel Bay](/town/mossel-bay) and [Nature's Way Farm Stall](/natures-way-farm-stall-plettenberg-bay) on The Crags both have animals and space to run.

## Easy crowd-pleasers

When you need a guaranteed win, the Spur chain is family HQ — [Geronimo Spur](/geronimo-spur-george) in George and [Lynx Creek Spur](/lynx-creek-spur-mossel-bay) in [Hartenbos](/town/hartenbos) have play areas and menus kids actually eat. For relaxed central dining, [Salt & Petal](/salt-petal-knysna) in [Knysna](/town/knysna) and the hearty [SKAAPSTERT Kos-en-Koffiestal](/skaapstert-kos-en-koffiestal-mossel-bay) in [Great Brak River](/town/great-brak-river) are reliable.

## Beachfront and casual

Beachfront [Ilmazzo](/ilmazzo-mossel-bay) in Hartenbos and the laid-back [Kahuna's](/kahuna-s-sedgefield) in [Sedgefield](/town/sedgefield) keep things easy with pizzas, burgers and sea air.

## Tips for eating out with kids

Aim for an early dinner (5:30–6:30pm) to beat the crowds, choose places with outdoor space, and check our [dog-friendly guide](/blog/dog-friendly-restaurants-garden-route) too if the family pet is along for the ride.
