Half the joy of eating on the Garden Route is *where* you're sitting. A good view turns a plate of fish into a memory. These are the tables worth booking for the scenery alone — see them all on our [sea-view restaurants](/eat/sea-view) page.

## At the water's edge

[East Head Café](/east-head-cafe-knysna-knysna) is the classic — right at the mouth of the [Knysna](/town/knysna) lagoon where the sea surges between the Heads. On Thesen Island, [Nest Food Bar](/nest-food-bar-knysna) does cocktails over the water. In [Victoria Bay](/town/victoria-bay), the laid-back [New Wave Village Café](/new-wave-village-cafe-victoria-bay) looks straight onto one of the country's best surf breaks.

## Clifftops and high views

For drama, [Pinnacle Point](/pinnacle-point-restaurant-mossel-bay) sits on a clifftop above [Mossel Bay](/town/mossel-bay), and [Hilltop Boutique Hotel](/hilltop-boutique-hotel-victoria-bay) gazes down over Victoria Bay's surf. In [Hartenbos](/town/hartenbos), beachfront [Ilmazzo](/ilmazzo-mossel-bay) pairs Italian plates with ocean views.

## River and lagoon views

It's not all open ocean. [Emily Moon](/emily-moon-plettenberg-bay) overlooks the Bitou River wetlands near [Plett](/town/plettenberg-bay) — one of the loveliest settings on the route — and the [Oak Leaf](/oak-leaf-restaurant-gourmet-deli-coffee-bar-knysna) estate near Belvidere offers calm water views away from the crowds.

## Beach feasts

For sea views with your toes in the sand, [De Vette Mossel](/de-vette-mossel-grootbrak-beach-seafood-restaurant-experience-mossel-bay) at [Great Brak River](/town/great-brak-river) is hard to beat.
