Plettenberg Bay — Plett to everyone who knows it — is the Garden Route's glamorous beach town, and its restaurants match the postcard. This is where you come for a long lunch with a view and a dinner worth dressing up for. Here are the standouts, plus [every restaurant in Plett](/town/plettenberg-bay) with current details.

## Special-occasion dining

[Emily Moon](/emily-moon-plettenberg-bay) is the name locals give first — a river-estate restaurant overlooking the Bitou wetlands, perfect for a milestone meal. For oceanfront elegance, [Amelia's at The Plettenberg](/amelia-s-at-the-plettenberg-plettenberg-bay) delivers seafood and sweeping bay views, while [The Old Rectory](/the-old-rectory-hotel-and-spa-plettenberg-bay) offers refined meals in a historic garden setting. See more [fine dining](/eat/fine-dining) and [date-night spots](/eat/date-night).

## Everyday favourites

Plett does casual brilliantly. [Café Civara](/cafe-civara-plettenberg-bay) and [Eleven Coffee Shop](/eleven-coffee-shop-plett-plettenberg-bay) anchor the Main Street coffee scene, while [The Local](/the-local-plettenberg-bay-plettenberg-bay) pours locally-roasted coffee with wholesome breakfasts. Just east of town, [Nice Neighbour Cafe](/nice-neighbour-cafe-plettenberg-bay) is a farm-fresh institution that draws a crowd for good reason.

## Out toward The Crags

It's worth driving east. [Bramon Wine Estate](/bramon-wine-estate-plettenberg-bay) is the Garden Route's pioneering vineyard — tapas and bubbly under the vines, and [dog-friendly](/eat/dog-friendly) too. Stock up on home-baked goods at [Nature's Way Farm Stall](/natures-way-farm-stall-plettenberg-bay) on the way to [Nature's Valley](/town/natures-valley).

## Casual bites and cocktails

For something relaxed, [Bao Wow](/bao-wow-plettenberg-bay-plettenberg-bay) does Asian street food at The Market, [Seakrit Spot](/seakrit-spot-plettenberg-bay) handles hearty breakfasts and smoothies, and [Sotto](/sotto-mediterranean-restaurant-cocktail-lounge-plettenberg-bay) brings Mediterranean plates and cocktails into the evening.

## Nearby

Plett pairs naturally with [Knysna](/town/knysna) (30 minutes west) and [Storms River](/town/storms-river) for the Tsitsikamma. Browse Plett by [seafood](/eat/seafood), [sea views](/eat/sea-view) or [coffee shops](/eat/coffee-shop), or read the [full Garden Route dining guide](/blog/best-restaurants-garden-route).
