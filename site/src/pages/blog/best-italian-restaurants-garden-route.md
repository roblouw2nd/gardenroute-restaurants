South Africa's Garden Route has quietly grown a proper Italian scene — family-run trattorias, wood-fired ovens and beachfront pasta with ocean views. More than 30 restaurants on the route serve Italian; these are the standouts.

## The beachfront star: Ilmazzo, Hartenbos

[Ilmazzo](/ilmazzo-mossel-bay) is the route's rare perfect score — 5★ across its Google reviews — serving authentic Italian right on the Hartenbos beachfront. Ocean views with your pasta is a hard combination to argue with, and nobody on the route does it better.

## George: the Italian heartland

The biggest town on the route has the deepest Italian bench. [Polpetta Ristorante](/polpetta-ristorante-george-george) is the trattoria proper — handmade pasta, wood-fired pizza, 4.8★ from nearly 200 reviews. [Portobello Italian Kitchen](/portobello-italian-kitchen-george) in George Central has racked up 370+ reviews doing the classics well, while [La Famiglia](/la-famiglia-george) brings homemade pasta to the Kingswood Golf Estate. For pizza specifically, [K-risp](/k-risp-pizza-george) (4.9★) and Neapolitan-style [Holy Dough](/holy-dough-pizza-george-george) fight it out — see the full [George Italian list](/town/george/italian).

## Small-town gems

In Great Brak River, [The Famiglia](/the-famiglia-mossel-bay) is the kind of family-run Italian café every village deserves — fresh pastries, proper coffee, 4.9★. Sedgefield's [La Bosca](/la-bosca-restaurant-wedding-venue-garden-route-knysna-sedgefield) blends Italian and Portuguese cooking in a garden setting that also moonlights as a wedding venue.
