Breakfast might be the Garden Route's best meal. Cool mornings, ocean air and a culture of slow weekends make it the time to linger over good coffee and a proper plate. Here's where to start the day — and the [full breakfast and brunch list](/eat/breakfast-brunch).

## With a view

Few breakfasts beat [East Head Café](/east-head-cafe-knysna-knysna) at the [Knysna](/town/knysna) lagoon mouth — go early for a window seat. In [Mossel Bay](/town/mossel-bay), [Coffee@Work on Point Road](/coffee-work-oppipunt-mossel-bay) does fresh breakfasts with sea air.

## Farm-fresh and wholesome

Just outside [Plett](/town/plettenberg-bay), [Nice Neighbour Cafe](/nice-neighbour-cafe-plettenberg-bay) is a farm-fresh favourite that draws a weekend crowd, and [Nature's Way Farm Stall](/natures-way-farm-stall-plettenberg-bay) on The Crags is a home-baked institution. In [Sedgefield](/town/sedgefield), [Liquid & Leaf](/liquid-leaf-liqid-new-location-sedgefield) and [Little Prince](/little-prince-sedgefield) handle the slow-town brunch beautifully.

## Best of the rest

[The Commonage](/the-commonage-wilderness) and [Jammwich](/jammwich-wilderness) make [Wilderness](/town/wilderness) a breakfast destination, while [Salt & Petal](/salt-petal-knysna) is the family-friendly central pick in Knysna. In [George](/town/george), [Pause Coffee @ The Barn on Montagu](/pause-coffee-the-barn-on-montagu-george) is a lovely barn-setting start.

## Make a morning of it

Pair breakfast with a market — [Sedgefield's Saturday markets](/blog/best-restaurants-sedgefield) are the route's best. Browse all [breakfast and brunch](/eat/breakfast-brunch) and [coffee shops](/eat/coffee-shop), or see the [full Garden Route guide](/blog/best-restaurants-garden-route).
