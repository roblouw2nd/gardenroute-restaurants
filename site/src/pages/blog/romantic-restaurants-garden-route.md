Whether it's an anniversary, a proposal or just a night that deserves a little effort, the Garden Route does romance well. Think candlelit estates, oceanfront tables and that golden Garden Route light. Here are the most romantic places to eat — and the [full date-night list](/eat/date-night).

## River and estate settings

[Emily Moon](/emily-moon-plettenberg-bay) near [Plett](/town/plettenberg-bay) is the route's most-recommended special-occasion restaurant, set above the Bitou River wetlands — book a sunset table. The historic [Old Rectory](/the-old-rectory-hotel-and-spa-plettenberg-bay) offers elegant dining in a tranquil garden setting.

## Oceanfront elegance

[Amelia's at The Plettenberg](/amelia-s-at-the-plettenberg-plettenberg-bay) pairs refined seafood with sweeping bay views, while [Pinnacle Point](/pinnacle-point-restaurant-mossel-bay) in [Mossel Bay](/town/mossel-bay) serves dinner from a dramatic clifftop. For a quieter view, [Hilltop Boutique Hotel](/hilltop-boutique-hotel-victoria-bay) overlooks [Victoria Bay](/town/victoria-bay).

## Boutique-hotel fine dining

In [George](/town/george), [The Hawthorn Boutique Hotel](/the-hawthorn-boutique-hotel-george) and the historic [Deacon House](/the-deacon-house-george) both deliver polished, intimate evenings — among the best [fine dining](/eat/fine-dining) on the route.

## Make it effortless

Book ahead (especially in summer), request a window or garden table when you reserve, and time it for sunset — the Garden Route's best romantic asset is free.
